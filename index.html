Why this matters

Awareness only works when it reaches her.

For women displaced by war, a routine check-up or a conversation about warning signs can feel impossible. Breast cancer found early is far more treatable, but only if women know what to look for and where to turn.

“Meaningful health awareness goes beyond sharing information. It requires building the knowledge and skills to communicate effectively and responsibly within the community.”

That is the idea behind this campaign. Dr. Sameeh Abu Habel opened the training on behalf of Watan – The Seeds of Love – USA. Participants are learning to teach breast self-examination, to explain why early detection matters, and to help women reach timely medical care.

Once trained, these educators carry that knowledge into the communities that need it most, including the displacement camps across Gaza.
